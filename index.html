About Me

My research focuses on turbulent transport processes in the atmospheric boundary layer, primarily based on direct observations. A central theme of my work is understanding the mechanisms governing energy and greenhouse gas exchange between the land surface and the atmosphere, particularly over heterogeneous environments such as urban and mountainous regions.
